# What CPI and PPI releases do to the ES tape

**Verdict: DESCRIPTIVE**

## Data
15 measured releases (7 CPI, 8 PPI), Apr 2025 – Sep 2026, against 158 non-release control sessions on the same contracts.

## Results
| Window | Range vs control | Volume vs control |
|---|---|---|
| First minute | 10.7× | 13.4× |
| 07:30–08:29 | 2.7× | 2.05× |
| Regular hours | 1.02× | 1.04× |

- At least one release-hour extreme never revisited in RTH: 73% of release days vs 33% of normal days.
- First-minute direction matched the surprise sign on 10 of 14; RTH close agreed with it on 6 of 14.
- Chasing the first-minute impulse to 08:29: 4 of 15 wins, median −2.0 points.
- CPI hits its hour extreme in minute 1 (median); PPI builds to minute 14.

**Data.** Market data is licensed (CQG via Quantower, Databento, MenthorQ) and is not included. This folder holds code, pre-registrations and derived statistics only. Every script reads from a local data path set in its header.
